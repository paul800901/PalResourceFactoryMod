v0.3.20 fixes a packaging error in v0.3.19 that could leave the Egg Resource Processor's old native module disabled on Palworld 1.0.5. The building could still appear normally, but eggs stayed in its container and could be removed. This update rebuilds the two native modules from current source before packaging. Local logs confirm processor startup, attachment and power; the reporter's complete egg-to-material test is pending. Update the mod and fully restart the game.

v0.3.20 修正 v0.3.19 的封包錯誤：舊版「帕魯資源分解機」原生模組在 Palworld 1.0.5 會被停用，因此建築外觀仍正常，但蛋會留在容器內且可取回。本次更新會在封包前從目前原始碼重新建置兩個原生模組。本機日誌已確認處理器啟動、掛載與供電；回報者的完整蛋到材料測試尚待確認。更新模組後請完整重啟遊戲。

[h1]Pal Resource Factory[/h1]

Two machines automate incubation and butchering, using native game results and dropping materials in front. They exchange materials, electricity, eggs and cake for convenience; this is not a free resource generator or a way to keep offspring.

[h2]Machines, power and costs[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Egg Resource Processor:[/b] 54 egg slots; automatic incubation (+50%) and butchering. Power: 200/s normally, 400/s while incubating.
[*][b]Resource Breeding Facility:[/b] Assign one male and one female and supply cake. Automatically breeds, incubates (+100%) and butchers offspring, not the assigned parents. Power: 500/s normally, 1000/s while incubating.
[*]Both pause without electricity and resume when power returns. Materials drop in front for pickup or transport by Pals. Native results are used without copied drop tables or extra quantity/rarity.
[/list]
Both technologies unlock at level 1 to avoid overlapping other entries without rebuilding the technology interface. The processor costs 3 regular technology points; the facility costs 4. Construction work is 500 for each.
[list]
[*][b]Processor materials:[/b] Wooden Planks ×10, Paldium Fragments ×30, Ingots ×20, Nails ×20.
[*][b]Breeding facility materials:[/b] Refined Ingots ×30, Circuit Boards ×10, Cryogenic Coolant ×10, Nails ×30.
[/list]

[h2]Installation[/h2]
[olist]
[*]Subscribe to [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3625223587]UE4SS Experimental (Palworld)[/url], [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3625280368]PalSchema[/url] and this mod.
[*]Enable all three in Mod Management, then fully restart the game.
[*]Unlock a machine on the technology page and press B to build it in your base.
[/olist]

[h2]Irreversible inputs and removal[/h2]
[b]Deposited eggs, offspring being processed and consumed cake cannot be returned. Only unused stock cake is recoverable.[/b]
Back up your save before removing the mod. Dismantle its buildings while the mod is enabled; dismantling destroys the remaining processing contents.

[h2]Languages and compatibility[/h2]
The interface follows the game setting and includes 17 languages. Technology, build-menu, building and inventory text are localized; model lettering is unchanged. Language layouts and translations have not all been verified.
Version 0.3.20 targets Windows Steam Palworld 1.0.5.102999. It fixes a v0.3.19 packaging error that could leave the processor's old native module disabled: the building remained visible, but eggs were not locked or processed. The current native modules are rebuilt before packaging. Local startup, attachment, power and DirectX 12 materials are confirmed; the reporter's complete egg-to-material test, FPS, long-running operation, multiplayer, dedicated servers and a fresh Workshop installation remain unverified. Existing blocked eggs may need individual diagnosis. Back up your save.

[h1]帕魯資源工廠[/h1]

兩台機器自動孵化與支解，使用遊戲原版結果，材料掉落在機器前方。以建材、電力、蛋與蛋糕換取省力；不是免費資源產生器，也不是用來保留後代的育種工具。

[h2]機器、耗電與建造成本[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]蛋資源處理機：[/b]54 格蛋倉；自動孵化（速度加成 50%）並支解。一般耗電 200／秒，孵化時 400／秒。
[*][b]資源繁殖場：[/b]指派一雄一雌親代並供應蛋糕，自動配種、孵化（速度加成 100%）並支解後代，不會支解指派的親代。一般耗電 500／秒，孵化時 1000／秒。
[*]兩台都會在停電時暫停、復電後續行；材料掉落在機器前方，可自行拾取或由帕魯搬運。使用原版結果，不複製掉落表，也不額外提高數量或稀有率。
[/list]
兩座科技刻意配置於等級 1，以避免與其他項目重疊，不必重做科技介面。處理機需要普通科技點 3 點，繁殖場需要 4 點；兩台建築工作量皆為 500。
[list]
[*][b]處理機建材：[/b]木板 ×10、帕魯礦碎片 ×30、金屬錠 ×20、釘子 ×20。
[*][b]繁殖場建材：[/b]精煉金屬錠 ×30、電路板 ×10、極低溫冷卻介質 ×10、釘子 ×30。
[/list]

[h2]安裝[/h2]
[olist]
[*]訂閱 [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3625223587]UE4SS Experimental (Palworld)[/url]、[url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3625280368]PalSchema[/url] 與本模組。
[*]在遊戲模組管理中啟用三個項目，再完整重啟遊戲。
[*]到科技頁解鎖機器，按 B 在據點內建造。
[/olist]

[h2]不可返還的投入與移除[/h2]
[b]已投入的蛋、處理中的後代與已消耗的蛋糕都不能取回；只有尚未消耗的庫存蛋糕可以取回。[/b]
移除前請先備份存檔，並在模組仍啟用時拆除自訂建築；拆除會銷毀剩餘的處理內容。

[h2]語言與相容性[/h2]
介面自動跟隨遊戲設定，包含 17 種語言。科技、建造選單、建築與庫存文字已本地化；機身印字不變。各語言排版與翻譯尚未全部逐一驗證。
v0.3.20 目標為 Windows Steam 版 Palworld 1.0.5.102999。它修正 v0.3.19 的封包錯誤：處理器的舊原生模組可能被停用，建築仍會顯示，但蛋不會被鎖定或處理。目前的兩個原生模組現在會在封包前重新建置。本機已確認啟動、掛載、供電與 DirectX 12 材質；回報者的完整蛋到材料測試、FPS、長時間運轉、多人、專用伺服器及全新工作坊安裝尚未驗證；既有卡住的蛋可能需要個別診斷。請先備份存檔。

[h1]Pal Resource Factory[/h1]
[i]English first. 繁體中文說明在下半部。[/i]

[h2]Purpose[/h2]
Two machines for turning eggs and bred offspring into resources with less repetitive work. Designed for midgame shortages of Pal Souls or specific materials, they automate incubation and butchering.
You trade materials, electricity, eggs and cake for convenience. This is not a free resource generator or a breeding tool for keeping offspring. Output uses the game's native butchering results: no extra drop quantity or rarity bonus, and no guarantee that every Pal drops the material you want.

[h2]The two machines[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Egg Resource Processor:[/b] 54 egg slots. Deposit eggs for automatic incubation (+50% speed) and butchering. Power: 200/s normally, 400/s while incubating.
[*][b]Resource Breeding Facility:[/b] Assign one male and one female parent and supply cake. Automatically breeds, incubates (+100% speed) and butchers the offspring, not the assigned parents. Power: 500/s normally, 1000/s while incubating.
[*]Both pause without electricity and resume when power returns. Materials drop in front for pickup or transport by Pals.
[/list]

[h2]Unlocks and deliberate costs[/h2]
Both technologies are available at level 1. The processor costs 3 regular technology points; the breeding facility costs 4. This deliberate placement avoids overlapping other technology entries without resizing or reworking the game's technology interface.
The practical requirements are materials and sustained power. The breeding facility automates more steps, so it costs more to build and run. Incubation uses more power than normal operation; automation saves repeated actions, not running costs.
[list]
[*][b]Processor construction:[/b] Wooden Planks ×10, Paldium Fragments ×30, Ingots ×20, Nails ×20.
[*][b]Breeding facility construction:[/b] Refined Ingots ×30, Circuit Boards ×10, Cryogenic Coolant ×10, Nails ×30.
[/list]

[h2]Installation[/h2]
[olist]
[*]Subscribe to [url=https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=3625223587]UE4SS Experimental (Palworld)[/url], [url=https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=3625280368]PalSchema[/url] and this mod.
[*]Enable all three in the game's Mod Management, then fully restart the game.
[*]Unlock the desired machine on the technology page, then press B to build it in your base.
[/olist]

[h2]Irreversible inputs and uninstalling[/h2]
[b]Deposited eggs and offspring being processed cannot be taken back. Consumed cake is not returned; only unused stock cake is recoverable.[/b]
Before uninstalling, back up your save and dismantle the mod's buildings while the mod is still enabled. Dismantling destroys the remaining processing contents.

[h2]Languages and compatibility[/h2]
17 interface languages follow the game setting automatically; fully restart after changing the game language. This covers technology, B build-menu and building/inventory text. Model lettering is unchanged. Not all language layouts and translations have been individually verified.
Version 0.3.17 targets Windows Steam Palworld 1.0.4.102642. This update lets unstarted breeding-facility processing resume after a normal world exit while retaining already calculated drops, and reduces temporary object creation. Local regression tests pass; the author confirmed continued processing after individual recovery of five old stuck eggs. Existing eggs already blocked by an older interrupted operation may still need individual diagnosis; updating alone does not repair every old blocked egg. Long-running and FPS validation remain incomplete. Multiplayer, dedicated servers and other game versions are not supported by this preview. After game updates, wait for compatibility confirmation before re-enabling this mod.

[h1]帕魯資源工廠[/h1]

[h2]設計目的[/h2]
提供兩台自動化設備，將蛋與配種後代轉為資源，減少重複操作。主要針對遊戲中期缺乏帕魯之魂或特定材料的情況，省去反覆手動孵化與支解的步驟。
這是用建材、電力、蛋與蛋糕換取省力，不是免費生產資源，也不是用來保留後代的育種設備。產物採用遊戲當下的原版支解結果，不額外提高掉落數量或稀有率，也不保證每種帕魯都能產出你需要的材料。

[h2]兩台設備[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]蛋資源處理機：[/b]54 格蛋倉。投入蛋後自動孵化（速度加成 50%）並支解。一般耗電 200／秒，孵化中 400／秒。
[*][b]資源繁殖場：[/b]指派一雄一雌親代並供應蛋糕，自動配種、孵化（速度加成 100%）並支解後代；不會支解指派的親代。一般耗電 500／秒，孵化中 1000／秒。
[*]兩台設備都會在停電時暫停、復電後續行。產出的材料掉落在機器前方，可自行拾取或由帕魯搬運。
[/list]

[h2]科技解鎖與成本設計[/h2]
兩台科技皆可於等級 1 解鎖；蛋資源處理機需要普通科技點 3 點，資源繁殖場需要 4 點。這是刻意的配置，為了避免與其他科技項目重疊，不必擴大或重做遊戲的科技介面。
實際使用門檻在於建材與持續供電。資源繁殖場整合更多自動化步驟，因此建造與運轉成本較高；孵化期間的耗電也高於平時。省下的是重複操作，不是運轉成本。
[list]
[*][b]蛋資源處理機建材：[/b]木板 ×10、帕魯礦碎片 ×30、金屬錠 ×20、釘子 ×20。
[*][b]資源繁殖場建材：[/b]精煉金屬錠 ×30、電路板 ×10、極低溫冷卻介質 ×10、釘子 ×30。
[/list]

[h2]安裝[/h2]
[olist]
[*]訂閱 [url=https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=3625223587]UE4SS Experimental (Palworld)[/url]、[url=https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=3625280368]PalSchema[/url] 與本模組。

[h2]不可返還的投入與移除方式[/h2]
[b]已投入的蛋與處理中的後代無法取回。已消耗的蛋糕不會返還；只有尚未消耗的庫存蛋糕可以取回。[/b]
移除模組前，請先備份存檔，並在模組仍啟用時拆除這兩種建築。拆除會銷毀機器內剩餘的處理內容。

[h2]語言與相容性[/h2]
介面提供 17 種語言，自動跟隨遊戲設定；切換遊戲語言後請完整重啟。涵蓋科技、B 建造選單與建築／庫存介面文字，機身印字維持不變。各語言的排版與翻譯尚未全部逐一驗證。
目前版本 0.3.17，適用目標為 Windows Steam 版 Palworld 1.0.4.102642。本次修正資源繁殖場在正常退出世界後，尚未執行的結算無法續作的問題，保留已算出的材料，並減少暫時物件建立。已通過本機回歸測試；作者將五顆舊卡蛋個別復原後，已確認可以繼續處理。舊版中斷後已被鎖住的蛋仍可能需要個別診斷，單純更新不會自動修復所有舊卡蛋。長時間與 FPS 驗證尚未完成。本預覽版不支援多人、專用伺服器及其他遊戲版本。遊戲更新後，請等候相容性確認再啟用本模組。
